    Motion

    The surface moves very slowly, like water. Turn it off when you need it still.

    Bring everything here

    Paste a dump from Notes, Notion, anywhere. One thought per line. #thread files a line. Start a line with ! to hold it.

    Carry it between devices

    This file keeps its own memory on whichever device opens it. Export here, import there.

    Let go

    Thoughts you released. Nothing is gone until you delete it.

    How it works

    The home screen is a surface, not a list. It never scrolls. What you see is what your mind is holding right now: everything you've held, the thoughts you've said yes to most, whatever arrived in the last day, and a few older ones resurfaced at random so nothing is forgotten by accident.

    A new thought arrives small, near where you typed it. It stays small until you tell it otherwise.

    Tap a thought and say +1. That's the whole system. Every time you see it and it's still a good idea, +1. Thoughts grow with each one and move toward the centre. Thoughts you never come back to drift out to the edges and eventually leave the surface, though they're always in All.

    Hold keeps a thought on the surface permanently, with a dot. Thoughts that share a thread drift toward each other over the day; thoughts with more +1s settle toward the centre.

    Tap a thread and the surface filters to it. Tap more than one: any shows thoughts carrying at least one of them, all shows only thoughts carrying every one, and the counts beside the other threads update to show where the overlaps are. List opens everything as a scrolling list.

    A small superscript on a thought is how many open steps it holds. Finish them and it goes quiet.

    Resurface deals a fresh hand of older thoughts. Write #music inside a thought to thread it. Enter keeps. / jumps to the box.
